About us · Indoors vs. courtyard

The lepak vibe

We started Pacito because PJ needed somewhere to hang out after dark that isn't a mamak or a bar. Good coffee, slow records, no alcohol, and nobody rushing you out.

Indoor Vinyl Lounge

Warm lamp glow, a record spinning, and Shut the Box on the table. Grab a corner and stay a while.

  • Vinyl
  • Tabletop games
  • Lamp-lit

An open grassy courtyard under fairy-lit trees, picnic mats laid out for sunset and stargazing hangs.
